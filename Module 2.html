Introduction to embedded systems

What is an Embedded System?
An embedded system is a computer system designed to perform a specific function or set of functions within a larger system.
Unlike general-purpose computers (like laptops or smartphones), embedded systems are special-purpose, optimized for efficiency, speed, and reliability.

Key Characteristics of Embedded Systems
  • Special-purpose: Designed for a specific task or application (e.g. controlling a washing machine motor).

  • Real-time operation: Many systems must respond within strict timing constraints (e.g. airbags in cars).

  • Compact and efficient: Often use limited memory, processing power, and energy.

  • Often embedded in hardware: Integrated into devices, not visible as standalone systems.


Components of an Embedded System
  1. Microcontroller / Microprocessor
    – Heart of the system (e.g. ARM Cortex, AVR, ESP32, STM32)
    – Executes program instructions

  2. Memory
    – RAM: Temporary data storage
    – ROM/Flash: Stores firmware (code)

  3. Input/Output Interfaces
    – Sensors, buttons, serial ports, displays

  4. Power Supply
    – Battery, USB, or regulated power input

  5. Software (Firmware)
    – Written in C, C++, or assembly; compiled and loaded into ROM

Benefits and Challenges
Benefits:
– Reliable, fast, and efficient
– Small and low power
– Optimized for the task

Challenges:
– Limited resources (memory, processing)
– Difficult to update once deployed
– Requires knowledge of both software and hardware

Embedded Systems & IoT
Embedded systems are at the core of IoT (Internet of Things) — small devices that sense, process, and communicate.
ESP32, for example, is a popular embedded system in the IoT world due to its built-in Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.

Summary
An embedded system is a tiny, efficient brain embedded inside a product or device, designed to do one job really well.
Whether it’s a smartwatch tracking your steps, a thermostat adjusting your room temperature, or a car preventing skids — embedded systems make modern life smarter and safer.
